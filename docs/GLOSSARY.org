#+TITLE: Glossary
#+DESCRIPTION: For language of contributors and agents both.

* Lumina
Lumina is a church presentation application: an operator drives a planned service from a Lumina-controlled screen while the audience sees Lumina's presentation.

** Decisions
Look in [[file:adr/][docs/adr/]] for decisions. Most of the time, the feature currently being implemented should be recorded there. Current feature:

- [[file:adr/20261125:0001-live-service-operator-workspace.org][Live Service Operator Workspace]]: live navigation, safe editing, blackout, and operator awareness.

** Language

*** People

*Operator*:
The person running Lumina during a service.
_Avoid_: User, presenter

*** Service and content

*Service*:
A single live occasion run from a service plan, such as a Sunday morning gathering.
_Avoid_: Event, meeting, session

*Service plan*:
The ordered list of service items prepared for a service.
_Avoid_: Playlist, setlist, running order, queue

*Service item*:
One unit of presentable content in a service plan, such as a song, video, scripture reading, or sermon.
_Avoid_: Slide, clip, entry, cue

*Slide*:
A single displayable unit of content within a service item.
_Avoid_: Page, frame, screen

*Section*:
A named subdivision of a service item that groups its slides, such as a verse or chorus.
_Avoid_: Part, segment, block

*** Presentation

*Presentation*:
Lumina's visual presentation shown to the audience.
_Avoid_: Show, display, output window

*Live item*:
The service item currently being presented.
_Avoid_: Current item, active item, now playing

*Live slide*:
The slide currently being presented to the audience.
_Avoid_: Current slide

*Live output*:
What the audience currently sees from Lumina, including solid black during a blackout.
_Avoid_: Screen, projector, what's up

*Preview*:
Content the operator is inspecting or preparing, which may not be what the audience sees.
_Avoid_: Next slide, selection, staged

*Blackout*:
A temporary covering of the presentation with solid black; it affects Lumina's presentation only, never OBS.
_Avoid_: Blank screen, clear, mute, stop

*Show output*:
The action that reveals the current live slide again without advancing the presentation.
_Avoid_: Unblank, resume, restore, unhide

*** Editing

*Unsaved edit*:
A change still being entered in an editor and not yet saved.
_Avoid_: Pending change

*Unapplied live changes*:
Saved corrections to the live item that have not been applied to its live output.
_Avoid_: Unsaved changes, pending changes, saved changes

*Apply to live*:
The explicit action that publishes unapplied live changes to the live output.
_Avoid_: Save, publish, push, update

*** OBS

*OBS*:
The external streaming application that receives Lumina's presentation as a source.
_Avoid_: Streaming software, broadcast app

*Active OBS scene*:
The OBS scene that OBS confirms it is currently showing.
_Avoid_: Current scene, requested scene, next scene
